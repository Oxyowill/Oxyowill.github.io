Flamingo

Graceful wader with pink plumage

Flamingos stand in shallow water and sweep the bill to filter tiny food. The pink color comes from pigments in shrimp and algae.

  • Very long legs and neck
  • Often rests while standing on one leg
  • Lives around salty lakes and lagoons
