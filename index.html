Welcome to Birds

Simple facts for new learners

Birds are warm blooded animals with feathers and wings. Many birds can fly. Some birds swim or glide. This site has three bird pages and a glossary. I use bold and emphasis to show small style changes.

You can learn more about birds at Audubon birds guide.
